## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an uncompromising, architectural presence tailored for an elite software engineering and design agency. It evokes raw technical capability, surgical precision, and avant-garde luxury. 

The aesthetic fuses **Monochrome Brutalism** with **Modern High-End Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Monolithic & Deliberate:** Unapologetic structural divisions, razor-sharp alignment, and high-density information architecture paired with deliberate expanses of negative space.
- **Inverted Obsidian Tone:** Pure cosmic blacks and graphite slabs strip away decorative fluff, letting code architecture, typographic hierarchy, and craftsmanship dominate.
- **Controlled Brutalism:** Structural borders and utilitarian metadata balance against organic, sculpted pill action points, delivering tactile human feedback within an engineered chassis.

## Colors

The palette operates in strict monochromatic purity. Value, contrast ratios, and structural opacity replace hue for communication.

- **Base Canvas:** `#050505` (deep void) for master backgrounds, stepping up to `#0a0a0a` for canvas scaffolding.
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Layered containers use `#121212` for primary sections and `#181818` for elevated interactives or secondary panels.
- **Structural Strokes:** Razor-thin boundaries set to `#222222` for standard grid divisions and `#2a2a2a` for interactive outlines and active hover states.
- **Typographic Scale:** Text hierarchy strictly commands `#ffffff` (headline pure white), `#e5e5e5` (body silver), `#a3a3a3` (secondary silver), and `#525252` (subtle structural labels and metadata).
- **Inverted Focus:** Primary points of interaction flip the palette completely—rendering stark `#ffffff` grounds with `#050505` ink.

## Typography

The typographic engine uses three distinct type families to establish an editorial technical hierarchy:
- **Headings (Space Grotesk):** Geometric and mechanical, yet editorial. Set with tight tracking to convey industrial authority.
- **Body & Prose (Geist):** Exceptionally clean, neutral grotesque designed for extreme readability on OLED displays.
- **System Labels, Badges & Meta (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced precision representing engineering rigour, terminal consoles, and brutalist metadata tags. Always set in uppercase with deliberate tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a disciplined modular grid paired with architectural hairline divisions:
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (breakpoint: 1280px+), 8-column grid on tablet (768px - 1279px), and 4-column structure on mobile (<768px).
- **Hairline Grids:** Major structural sections can connect via continuous 1px `#222222` border lines, avoiding floaty separation in favor of a blueprints-and-slabs feel.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8pt base grid for element layout and component padding, scaling exponentially for dramatic negative space transitions in case-study sections.

## Elevation & Depth

This system discards diffuse ambient drop shadows in favor of **structural depth, surface luminance tiers, and high-precision line work**:
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Pure Void `#050505`.
- **Layer 1 (Sub-base):** Architectural frame `#0a0a0a`.
- **Layer 2 (Containers & Cards):** Elevates to `#121212`, defined by a crisp 1px border stroke of `#222222`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Raised Hover):** Elevates to `#181818` with an active boundary stroke of `#2a2a2a`. Hover transitions do not lift via drop shadow; they brighten border strokes or initiate inverted high-contrast fills.
- **Zero Diffuse Shadows:** Depth is perceived through luminance contrast and physical architectural edge containment.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a signature dichotomy between **strict brutalist rectilinearity** and **sculpted ergonomic pills**:
- **Containers, Cards, Paneling, & Inputs:** Pure sharp geometry (`0px` border-radius) evoking monoliths, steel frames, and code architecture.
- **Intentional Contrast:** Call-to-action buttons, status badges, and interaction tags break the strict rectangular grid with full pill-radii (`9999px`), instantly signaling interactive affordance against the sharp structural containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** High-contrast solid `#ffffff` fill, `#050505` text, pill radius (`9999px`), JetBrains Mono or Geist medium, tracking compact. Hover: expands slightly or triggers inverse border outline (`#e5e5e5` stroke, `#121212` background, `#ffffff` text).
- **Secondary Brutalist:** Rectangular sharp outline button (`0px` radius), transparent background, 1px `#222222` stroke, `#ffffff` text. Hover: `#181818` background, `#2a2a2a` stroke.
- **Ghost Action:** Pure text with an interactive trailing arrow glyph (`→`), `#a3a3a3` transitioning to `#ffffff` on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Pill-shaped capsules (`9999px`), 1px border `#2a2a2a`, background `#0e0e0e`, text `#a3a3a3` in `label-mono-xs`. Features a 6px status dot (white or muted silver).
- **Metadata Tags:** Sharp (`0px`), uppercase `label-mono-xs`, `#181818` fill, `#222222` 1px border, `#e5e5e5` text.

### Cards & Work Containers
- Sharp rectangular slabs (`0px`), `#121212` background, 1px `#222222` stroke.
- Hover behavior: Subtle border luminance shift to `#2a2a2a` with image/content scale of 1.01x within clipped containers. No blur drop shadow.
- Interior layout embeds monospaced indices (e.g., `// 01. ARCHITECTURE`) anchored at the top-left or top-right.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Dark recessed background `#0a0a0a` or transparent underline/border box, sharp edges (`0px`), 1px `#222222` border.
- Text in Geist `#ffffff`, placeholder in `#525252`.
- Focus state: Border illuminates to crisp `#ffffff` with zero glow blur; label transitions to `label-mono-xs` in `#ffffff`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp square (`0px`), 1px `#2a2a2a` border, `#0a0a0a` fill. Active state fills pure `#ffffff` with a `#050505` checkmark icon.
- **Radios:** Dual concentric circles. Outer ring `#2a2a2a`, active state reveals `#ffffff` center dot.

### Lists & Data Tables
- Hairline horizontal rows divided by 1px `#222222`.
- Alternating hover states using subtle `#121212`.
- Monospaced numerals aligned right; primary labels in Space Grotesk or Geist aligned left.
